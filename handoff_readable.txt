## 7. Prediction inspection

Representative predictions were inspected using:

`scripts/inspect_predictions.py`

The inspection found:

- Exact matches: 4
- Valid but incorrect: 47
- Invalid/unsafe: 3

Observed behavior:

- Straightforward aggregation queries can be generated exactly.
- JOIN and aggregation structures are relatively strong.
- The primary weakness is semantic interpretation of more complex questions.
- Errors include incorrect filters, wrong entities/values, oversimplified queries,
  missing grouping/subqueries, and malformed or hallucinated SQL.
- Some outputs contain severe token-generation corruption.

This agrees with the aggregate evaluation results: SQL structure has been learned,
but exact semantic text-to-SQL accuracy remains limited.
